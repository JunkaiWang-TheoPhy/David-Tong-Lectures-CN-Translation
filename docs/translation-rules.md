# Translation Rules

1. Establish the course inventory from official sources. Record source URL, version, retrieval date, and checksum; distinguish teaching notes from books, exercises, and presentation slides.
2. Translate one complete pilot chapter before expanding to the full collection.
3. Preserve chapter structure, equation numbering, notation, footnotes, and cross-references.
4. Maintain shared terminology and course-specific exceptions. Record why an exception is necessary.
5. Label translator additions explicitly. Record suspected source errors for review rather than silently changing them.
6. Check PDF-derived equations, subscripts, superscripts, signs, and references against the original page.
7. Use automated checks for omissions and consistency, with human review for physical meaning and readable Chinese.
8. Track each chapter as source-verified, first-draft, physics-reviewed, language-reviewed, build-checked, or released. A machine draft alone is not a reviewed translation.
9. Publish derived material only under a verified applicable permission or license, retaining attribution.
10. Keep a revision trail connecting every translation to its source version.
